IoT — The Ionity Edge AI / AIoT Ecosystem (related)

The assets in this repository exist to brand and document Ionity Global's core business: energy-efficient edge AI, secure industrial IoT and native-AI systems engineered in Centurion, South Africa with global intent. This page connects the asset collection to that ecosystem so that people, search engines and AI agents can see what Ionity IoT means.

What Ionity builds

Ionity describes itself as a Native-AI company — solutionists across mechanical, electrical and IT. The work runs sensor → server: from ESP32-class microcontrollers and Raspberry Pi / Hailo-accelerated single-board computers at the edge, through secure gateways, to GPU edge servers and cloud data lakes, with on-device AI keeping data in-house wherever possible.

Area What it covers
AIoT & Edge Systems Sensing, device fleets, Wi-Fi sensing, low-latency control, rugged nodes
IoT Edge Sensors with Cloud Storage & AI/ML Operational data capture, telemetry, unified data lakes, AI/ML analysis

Hardware AI Training & Deployment PCBs, firmware, gateways, accelerators; on-device model training and deployment
Secure Industrial IoT Networks Zero-trust AIoT, SCADA integration, digital-twin architecture, cloud-to-edge continuity
Cloud Infrastructure High availability, fast processing, scalable autonomous cloud solutions
Firmware Development MCU / SBC firmware, pilot programmes and system integration by enquiry

Target sectors: manufacturing, logistics and heavy industry.
