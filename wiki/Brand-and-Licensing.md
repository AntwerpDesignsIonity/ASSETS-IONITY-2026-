### Usage rules

Use the marks as supplied from `brand/` or `business-pack/` without recolouring, stretching, rotating, outlining or adding effects. Keep clear space of at least the height of the "I" around the logo and prefer the transparent PNG or SVG on dark backgrounds. Do not combine the mark with another company's logo so that it implies partnership or endorsement, do not use it as part of your own product name or domain, and do not use the marks for anything that contradicts Policy 986 AED. Team business cards and personal signatures (`ASignature.png`, `Johanwilhelmvanantwerpesignatureionity.png`, `*Business card*`) identify named people and are **not** general-purpose assets.
